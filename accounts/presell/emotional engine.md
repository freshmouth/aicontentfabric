EMOTIONAL ENGINE

During the first 3 seconds the viewer should feel:

Curiosity.

During seconds 3-10:

Confusion.

"Wait... what's happening?"

During seconds 10-20:

Discovery.

"So that's what the system is doing."

During seconds 20-35:

Escalation.

"It also does THAT?"

During seconds 35-50:

Awe.

"This is actually impressive."

During the last 10 seconds:

Desire.

"I need this in my business."

Never explain this progression.

Create it entirely through visuals, pacing, ambient sound, and real-world consequences.
