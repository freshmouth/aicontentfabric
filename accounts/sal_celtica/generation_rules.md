# Sal Celtica Generation Rules

These rules apply to all future Sal Celtica V3 runs and scheduled posts.

## Social Description

- Do not make the post description a transcript or near-copy of the video script.
- The description should be contextual: it should frame the topic, tease the unresolved idea, and create an open loop that makes the viewer want the exact source/product.
- Keep the same CTA keyword used in the video. If the video says `Comenta SAL`, the post description must also use `Comenta SAL`.
- Do not introduce a different CTA, different keyword, or extra funnel promise.
- Keep captions short, native, and curiosity-driven. Avoid robotic summaries and repeated dialogue.
- Do not expose internal instructions, scene names, file names, escaped formatting, or prompt language.

Good pattern:

`Hay una diferencia enorme entre tomar agua y que tu cuerpo se sienta hidratado. La parte que casi nadie revisa esta en los minerales. Comenta SAL y te mando la que yo uso.`

Bad pattern:

`La neta: tomar mas agua no siempre soluciona la inflamacion. Si tu agua esta plana, sin minerales...`

The bad pattern is too close to the spoken video script and should be avoided.

## Product Handling

- Keep the product reveal curiosity-based in public captions.
- Do not over-explain the product in the description.
- Avoid medical certainty, cure claims, or disease claims.
- Keep the public language focused on minerals, hydration feel, food rituals, and simple daily use.
