## Purpose

Claire V2 uses a product-led educational UGC structure instead of the softer Claire V1 explainer format.
The video creates a curiosity loop, reveals a hidden mechanism, kills the obvious failed fix, introduces the
backend guide only after the mechanism is understood, then closes without sounding like an ad.

## Locked Runtime

- 0:00-0:08 Hook: open loop, no backend guide mention.
- 0:08-0:18 Problem: hidden cause the viewer has not connected.
- 0:18-0:28 Failed fix: why the obvious "healthy" choice keeps failing.
- 0:28-0:38 Mechanism: teach the scan/mechanism first, then introduce the guide as the practical vessel.
- 0:38-0:48 Honesty: realistic expectation or limitation.
- 0:48-0:50 Soft close: no hard selling, no CTA verbs in the spoken line.

## Claire V2 Adaptation

Claire is a fast-paced supermarket health investigator for women over 40. She exposes foods that look healthy
on the front of the package but behave differently once serving size, added sugar, protein, fiber, and oils are
checked.

Every video points back to the same backend ecosystem:

- Primary offer: 40+ Label Scan / supermarket guide.
- Comment keyword: LABEL unless a topic-specific guide is configured.
- DM bridge: send the concept-specific page from the same guide.

## Script Rules

- The backend guide does not appear before the mechanism block.
- Use sentence-case hook captions, never all caps.
- The spoken script should avoid hard CTA verbs.
- The social caption may contain the comment keyword for automation.
- One idea per sentence.
- No disease treatment, cure, detox, or guaranteed result claims.
- One honest limitation or realistic expectation must appear.
- The viewer is absolved: the problem is packaging, habit design, or missing information.

## Visual Rules

- Every scene must have a visual job, not just "Claire talks."
- Start with an object accusation in the first frame.
- Use real supermarket products or realistic store cues.
- Use label flips, serving-size checks, cart shots, product comparisons, and quick physical demonstrations.
- Keep Claire's identity, UGC realism, direct eye contact, and candid iPhone framing.
- No grotesque body visuals, fake medical exams, fake products, or Claire-branded packaging.

## First Test Variables

- Product name: 40+ Label Scan guide.
- Product category: digital supermarket label-scan guide.
- Core mechanism: protein, added sugar, and real serving size checked in under three seconds.
- Common problem: buying protein granola as a healthy snack but crashing or craving more later.
- Hidden cause: protein branding can hide small serving sizes, added sugar, and calorie-dense clusters.
- Failed alternative: trusting "protein" or "natural" claims on the front of the bag.
- Honest timeline: the first result is better cart decisions on the next grocery run, not an overnight body change.
- Usage ritual: check protein, added sugar, and serving size before the product enters the cart.
